# Orbit AX3000 Wi-Fi 6 Router — User Manual

## In the box

The package contains the Orbit AX3000 router, a 12 V 1.5 A power adapter, a one-metre Ethernet cable and a quick-start card. Keep the card: the default network name (SSID) and the default Wi-Fi password are printed on it and on the label underneath the router.

## Setting up

Connect the yellow WAN port on the back of the router to your modem or fibre terminal with the Ethernet cable, then plug in the power adapter. Wait about two minutes until the status LED turns solid white. Connect a phone or laptop to the default network and open the Orbit Home app, or visit http://orbit.home in a browser, to run the setup wizard. The wizard asks you to choose an administrator password, which is separate from the Wi-Fi password; it must be at least ten characters long.

If your internet provider uses PPPoE, the wizard asks for the user name and password supplied by your provider. For fibre connections that require a VLAN ID, enter it under Advanced → Internet → VLAN.

## Status LED

The status LED on the front panel shows what the router is doing.

| LED | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Solid white | Connected to the internet and working normally |
| Pulsing white | Starting up or installing a firmware update, do not unplug |
| Solid amber | Connected to the modem but no internet access |
| Blinking amber | No cable detected in the WAN port |
| Pulsing blue | Ready to pair with a mesh satellite or a WPS device |
| Solid red | Hardware fault; contact support |

## Wi-Fi bands

The AX3000 broadcasts on two bands. The 2.4 GHz band reaches further and passes through walls better but is slower and more crowded. The 5 GHz band supports speeds up to 2,402 Mbps but has a shorter range. By default both bands share one network name and Smart Connect moves each device to the better band automatically. If an older device has trouble connecting, turn off Smart Connect under Wireless → Bands and give the 2.4 GHz band its own name.

## Guest network

The guest network gives visitors internet access without letting them see your computers, printers or smart-home devices. Turn it on in the app under Guest Wi-Fi. You can set it to switch off automatically after 4, 8 or 24 hours.

## Parental controls

Create a profile for each family member, assign their devices to it, and set daily time limits or a bedtime schedule. Content filters block categories of websites. Time limits apply across all of a person's devices, so moving from a tablet to a laptop does not reset the allowance.

## Mesh expansion

For larger homes, add an Orbit MX1 satellite to extend coverage. Press the WPS button on the router until the LED pulses blue, then power on the satellite within two minutes; pairing completes automatically. Up to three satellites can be added to one router.

## Firmware updates

The router checks for firmware updates every night between 02:00 and 04:00 and installs them automatically if Auto Update is on. During an update the status LED pulses white and the network is unavailable for about three minutes. Never unplug the router while the LED is pulsing white, as this can corrupt the firmware.

## Resetting

To restart the router, unplug it for ten seconds. To restore factory settings, press and hold the recessed Reset button on the back with a paper clip for eight seconds until the status LED blinks amber quickly, then release it. A factory reset erases all your settings, including the administrator password, the Wi-Fi names and passwords, parental controls and port forwarding rules.

## Troubleshooting

If the LED is solid amber, restart your modem first and then the router. If speeds are slow, move the router to a central, raised position away from microwave ovens and cordless phones, which interfere with the 2.4 GHz band. If a device cannot find the 5 GHz network, check whether it supports 5 GHz; many low-cost smart plugs and cameras use 2.4 GHz only.

## Specifications

Wi-Fi standard: 802.11ax (Wi-Fi 6). Ports: one 2.5 Gbps WAN, three 1 Gbps LAN, one USB 3.0. Processor: quad-core 1.7 GHz. Encryption: WPA3-Personal and WPA2-Personal. Operating temperature: 0 °C to 40 °C. Warranty: two years from the date of purchase.
