# Northwind Robotics — Quarterly Report, Q3 FY2025

## Highlights

In the third quarter of fiscal year 2025 (July to September 2025), Northwind Robotics delivered record revenue of $158.2 million, up 22% year over year and slightly above the top of its guidance range. Gross margin was 42.6%, compared with 41.3% in the previous quarter. Operating income rose to $14.7 million. Cash and short-term investments stood at $226 million at the end of September.

## Revenue by segment

Warehouse Automation revenue was $104.8 million, up 17% year over year, driven by the large European deliveries that had been prepared during the second quarter. Healthcare Logistics revenue was $31.1 million, up 26%; the segment signed its first national contract, with a network of 42 hospitals in Canada that will deploy MedRunner units over eighteen months. Software and Services revenue grew 41% to $22.3 million.

FleetMind annual recurring revenue reached $71 million. During the quarter the company launched FleetMind Insights, an analytics add-on that predicts congestion in warehouse aisles and recommends charging schedules. More than 60 customers activated the add-on within its first eight weeks.

## Costs and margins

Cost of revenue was $90.8 million. Gross margin benefited from the higher software mix and from volume discounts on lidar sensors, partly offset by expedited freight costs of $1.4 million incurred to meet European delivery dates. Research and development expenses were $25.9 million. Sales, general and administrative expenses were $26.4 million, including $1.1 million for the company's first customer conference, Northwind Connect, held in Rotterdam.

No restructuring charges were recorded in the quarter; the consolidation of assembly into the Columbus facility was completed in August, two weeks ahead of schedule.

## Balance sheet and cash flow

Operating cash flow was $21.9 million and capital expenditure was $4.8 million. Inventory fell to $51.2 million as the stock built in the previous quarter was delivered. The company repaid $10 million of its term loan early, reducing total debt to $30 million. Days sales outstanding improved from 61 days to 54 days after the company moved several large customers to milestone-based billing.

## Operations

Robot shipments reached 2,390 units, the highest in the company's history. Average fleet uptime was 99.4%. The company began pilot production of the Carrier-9, a heavier robot with a lift capacity of 1,500 kilograms, aimed at automotive parts warehouses. A firmware defect that caused some Carrier-7 robots to stop at reflective floor markings was fixed with a remote update to all affected units within five days.

Headcount was 1,365 at the end of the quarter. The company opened an engineering office in Pune focused on simulation and fleet software, which employed 38 people by the end of September.

## Outlook

For the fourth quarter, management expects revenue of $162 million to $168 million. Full-year guidance was raised to $605 million to $615 million. The proposed tariffs on electronic components took effect in September; management now estimates their impact at about $2.1 million per quarter, below the earlier worst-case estimate, because the second drive-motor supplier is located outside the affected countries.

## Risks

Large warehouse orders remain lumpy, so revenue may vary significantly between quarters. Hospital customers increasingly ask for on-site data storage, which would require changes to how FleetMind is deployed. The concentration of revenue among the top customers declined slightly, with the five biggest accounts representing 34% of quarterly sales. A stronger US dollar would reduce reported revenue because a large share of sales is invoiced in euros.

## Note on non-GAAP measures

Adjusted operating income, which excludes stock-based compensation, was $18.2 million in the quarter. This measure is provided to help investors compare periods and should be read alongside results prepared under generally accepted accounting principles.
