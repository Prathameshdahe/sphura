# Northwind Robotics — Quarterly Report, Q2 FY2025

## Highlights

Northwind Robotics designs autonomous mobile robots for warehouses and hospitals. In the second quarter of fiscal year 2025 (April to June 2025), revenue grew 18% year over year to $142.6 million. Gross margin improved to 41.3% from 38.9% a year earlier, helped by lower battery costs and a higher share of software subscriptions. Operating income was $9.8 million, and the company ended the quarter with $212 million in cash and short-term investments.

## Revenue by segment

Warehouse Automation remained the largest segment with revenue of $96.4 million, up 14% year over year. Demand was strongest among third-party logistics providers in Europe, where two customers each ordered more than 300 units of the Carrier-7 robot. Healthcare Logistics revenue rose 31% to $28.9 million as hospitals expanded their use of the MedRunner platform for delivering medication and lab samples between wards. Software and Services, which includes the FleetMind orchestration subscription, contributed $17.3 million, up 29%.

Annual recurring revenue from software reached $63 million at the end of June, and net revenue retention for subscription customers was 118%.

## Costs and margins

Cost of revenue was $83.7 million. The improvement in gross margin came mainly from the switch to lithium iron phosphate battery packs in the Carrier-7, which lowered the bill of materials by about $1,100 per robot. Research and development expenses were $24.1 million, or 16.9% of revenue, reflecting continued investment in perception software and the next-generation lift module. Sales, general and administrative expenses were $25.0 million.

The company recorded a one-time charge of $2.3 million related to consolidating its two Ohio assembly sites into a single facility in Columbus. Excluding this charge, adjusted operating income would have been $12.1 million.

## Balance sheet and cash flow

Operating cash flow for the quarter was $15.6 million, and capital expenditure was $6.2 million, mostly for test equipment and the Columbus facility. Inventory increased to $58.0 million from $49.5 million at the end of the first quarter as the company built stock ahead of large deliveries scheduled for the third quarter. Total debt was unchanged at $40 million, consisting of a term loan that matures in 2028.

## Operations

Northwind shipped 2,140 robots in the quarter, compared with 1,780 in the same quarter of the previous year. Fleet uptime across all customer sites averaged 99.2%. Lead times for the Carrier-7 shortened from fourteen weeks to nine weeks after the company qualified a second supplier for drive motors.

Headcount reached 1,310 employees, of whom 46% work in engineering roles. Voluntary attrition over the trailing twelve months was 7.8%, below the industry average.

## Outlook

For the third quarter of fiscal 2025, management expects revenue between $150 million and $156 million and gross margin between 41% and 42%. The company reaffirmed its full-year revenue guidance of $590 million to $610 million. Management cautioned that new import tariffs on electronic components could raise costs by up to $3 million per quarter if they take effect as proposed.

## Risks

The company depends on a small number of large customers; its five largest customers accounted for 37% of revenue in the quarter. Delays in hospital procurement cycles could shift Healthcare Logistics revenue between quarters. The Carrier-7 relies on a single supplier for its lidar sensors, and a disruption at that supplier would delay shipments by several weeks. Currency movements also matter: roughly 40% of revenue is invoiced in euros, while most costs are incurred in US dollars.

## Note on non-GAAP measures

Adjusted operating income excludes restructuring charges and stock-based compensation. Management uses this measure to compare operating performance between periods, but it should be read together with, and not instead of, results reported under generally accepted accounting principles.
