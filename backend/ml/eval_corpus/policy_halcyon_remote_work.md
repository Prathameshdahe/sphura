# Halcyon Labs — Remote Work and Expenses Policy (HL-POL-014, version 3.2)

## 1. Purpose and scope

1.1 This policy explains how employees of Halcyon Labs may work away from the office and which related expenses the company reimburses. It applies to all permanent and fixed-term employees. Contractors are covered by the terms of their individual contracts instead.

1.2 This version replaces version 3.1 and takes effect on 1 April 2025.

## 2. Eligibility

2.1 Employees who have completed their probation period of three months may work remotely for up to three days per week, subject to their manager's approval.

2.2 Roles that require physical presence, such as laboratory technicians and facilities staff, are not eligible for regular remote work, but may work remotely for occasional administrative days.

2.3 Fully remote arrangements require approval from both the department head and the People team and are reviewed every twelve months.

## 3. Core hours and availability

3.1 Remote employees must be reachable during core hours, 10:30 to 16:00 in their team's primary time zone.

3.2 Employees should keep their calendar up to date and respond to messages within two working hours during core hours.

3.3 Team meetings that require attendance in person are announced at least five working days in advance.

## 4. Working from another country

4.1 Employees may work from outside their country of employment for up to 20 working days in any calendar year.

4.2 Requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 15 working days before travel so that tax and immigration implications can be checked.

4.3 Working abroad without approval may create tax liabilities for the employee and the company and may lead to disciplinary action.

## 5. Equipment

5.1 The company provides a laptop, a monitor and a headset to every employee who works remotely at least one day per week.

5.2 Employees may claim a one-time home-office allowance of up to ₹15,000 for a desk, chair or lighting, against receipts submitted within 60 days of purchase.

5.3 Company equipment must be returned within ten working days of the end of employment. Equipment that is not returned will be deducted from the final settlement at its depreciated value.

## 6. Internet and phone costs

6.1 Employees who work remotely at least two days per week receive a fixed connectivity allowance of ₹1,200 per month, paid with salary. No receipts are required.

6.2 Personal mobile phone costs are not reimbursed, except for international roaming charges incurred on approved business travel.

## 7. Travel to the office

7.1 Commuting costs to the employee's normal office are not reimbursed.

7.2 Fully remote employees who are asked to attend an in-person event receive travel by train in AC 2-tier, or economy class air travel when the journey exceeds eight hours by train, plus hotel accommodation up to ₹6,500 per night.

## 8. Claiming expenses

8.1 Expenses must be claimed through the Ledgerly app within 45 days of being incurred. Claims submitted later will be rejected unless the employee's manager approves an exception in writing.

8.2 Each claim must include an itemised receipt. A credit card slip alone is not sufficient.

8.3 Claims up to ₹10,000 are approved by the employee's manager; larger claims also require approval from the finance controller.

8.4 Approved claims are reimbursed in the next monthly payroll run.

## 9. Security

9.1 Company data may only be accessed on company devices or on personal devices enrolled in the device management system.

9.2 Employees must not work on confidential material in public places where screens can be overlooked, and must lock their screen whenever they step away.

9.3 Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the IT service desk within 24 hours.

## 10. Health and wellbeing

10.1 Remote employees are encouraged to take regular breaks and to keep a separate workspace where possible. The company's employee assistance programme, which offers confidential counselling, is available to remote and office-based staff alike.

10.2 Employees may request an ergonomic assessment of their home workspace once a year at no cost.

## 11. Review

11.1 The People team reviews this policy every year. Questions about the policy should be sent to the People team through the HR portal.
